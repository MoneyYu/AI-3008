Frequently Asked Questions - Returns and Exchanges

Q: What is your return window?
A: Unused items may be returned within 60 days of purchase for a full refund.

Q: Do I need a receipt?
A: A receipt or order confirmation from northwindtraders.example is required
for all returns and exchanges.

Q: Can I return online orders in store?
A: Yes. Online orders can be returned at any of our retail locations, including
our flagship store on Pine Street in Seattle.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five business days.
