About Northwind Traders

Northwind Traders is an outdoor equipment retailer founded in 1998 and
headquartered in Seattle, Washington. The company operates 42 retail stores
across the United States and Canada, with major distribution centers in Kent,
Washington and Columbus, Ohio.

Chief Executive Officer Priya Nair leads a team of more than 1,400 employees.
Under the direction of Chief Operating Officer Daniel Mendoza, the company has
expanded its e-commerce business to serve customers in Portland, Denver, and
Vancouver. Northwind Traders is committed to sustainable sourcing and donates
one percent of annual revenue to trail conservation programs.
