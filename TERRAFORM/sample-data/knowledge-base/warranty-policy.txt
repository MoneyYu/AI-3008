Northwind Traders Warranty Policy

All Northwind Traders branded tents, sleeping bags, and backpacks carry a
five-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.
Footwear and apparel are covered for one year.

To make a warranty claim, contact our support team in Seattle at
support@northwindtraders.example. Proof of purchase is required. Claims are
typically processed within ten business days. This warranty does not cover
damage caused by normal wear, improper use, or unauthorized repairs.
