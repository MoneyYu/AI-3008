Shipping and Delivery Policy

Northwind Traders ships to all 50 U.S. states and to Canada. Standard ground
shipping is free on orders over 75 dollars. Orders placed before 2:00 PM
Pacific Time ship the same business day from our Kent, Washington warehouse.

Expedited two-day shipping is available for an additional fee. International
customers in Vancouver and Toronto should allow seven to ten business days for
delivery. Tailwind Fulfillment LLC, based in Portland, Oregon, handles our
West Coast logistics.
