Trailblazer Membership Program

The Northwind Traders Trailblazer program rewards loyal customers with points
on every purchase. Members earn 5 points per dollar and receive early access to
seasonal sales.

Annual membership costs 40 dollars and includes free expedited shipping, a
20 percent discount on gear rentals, and two guided day hikes near Seattle or
Denver. Members who refer a friend receive a 15 dollar credit.
