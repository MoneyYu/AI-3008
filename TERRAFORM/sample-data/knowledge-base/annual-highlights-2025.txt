2025 Business Highlights

Northwind Traders reported strong growth in 2025. Total revenue reached 184
million dollars, up 12 percent from the prior year. The camping category led
sales, followed by apparel and climbing gear.

The company opened four new stores, including locations in Boulder, Colorado
and Spokane, Washington. E-commerce now accounts for 38 percent of total
revenue. Chief Executive Officer Priya Nair credited the company's loyalty
program and improved fulfillment through partner Tailwind Fulfillment LLC.
