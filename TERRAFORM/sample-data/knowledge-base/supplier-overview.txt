Supplier and Partner Overview

Northwind Traders works with a trusted network of suppliers. Adventure Works
Supply, based in Tempe, Arizona, provides tent poles and fabric. Fabrikam
Outfitters in Portland, Oregon supplies footwear and apparel for wholesale.

Logistics partner Contoso Logistics manages our Midwest distribution from
Columbus, Ohio, while Tailwind Fulfillment LLC handles West Coast orders from
Portland. Procurement is overseen by Daniel Mendoza and his team in Seattle.
