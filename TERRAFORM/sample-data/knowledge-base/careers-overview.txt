Careers at Northwind Traders

Northwind Traders is growing and frequently hires for roles in retail, supply
chain, product design, and software engineering. Our headquarters in Seattle,
Washington hosts most corporate positions, while store roles are available
across our 42 locations.

We offer competitive pay, an employee gear discount, and paid volunteer time
for trail conservation. To apply, visit the careers page at
northwindtraders.example or contact our recruiting team led by Hannah Brooks.
