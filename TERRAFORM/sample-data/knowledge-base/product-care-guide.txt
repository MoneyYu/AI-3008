Caring for Your Outdoor Gear

Proper care extends the life of your Northwind Traders equipment.

Tents: Always dry your tent completely before storage to prevent mildew. Clean
the floor with mild soap and water. Store loosely in a cool, dry place.

Sleeping bags: Store down bags uncompressed in a large cotton sack. Wash with a
specialized down cleaner no more than once per season.

Footwear: Remove dirt after each hike and condition leather boots regularly.
Avoid drying boots near direct heat, which can crack the soles.
