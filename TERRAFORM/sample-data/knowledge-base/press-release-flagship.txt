Northwind Traders Opens Expanded Flagship Store in Seattle

SEATTLE, WA - Northwind Traders today announced the grand reopening of its
flagship store on Pine Street following a six-month renovation. The expanded
30,000 square foot location features an indoor climbing wall, a gear repair
workshop, and a café operated in partnership with local roaster Cascade Coffee.

"This store reflects our commitment to the outdoor community," said Chief
Executive Officer Priya Nair. The company also announced plans to open new
locations in Denver, Colorado and Vancouver, British Columbia next year.
