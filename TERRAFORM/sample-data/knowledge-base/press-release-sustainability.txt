Northwind Traders Commits to Carbon-Neutral Shipping by 2027

SEATTLE, WA - Northwind Traders has pledged to make all of its shipping
operations carbon neutral by 2027. The initiative, led by Chief Operating
Officer Daniel Mendoza, includes a transition to electric delivery vehicles at
the company's Kent, Washington and Columbus, Ohio distribution centers.

The company is partnering with Contoso Logistics and Tailwind Fulfillment LLC
to optimize delivery routes and reduce emissions. Northwind Traders will also
invest in reforestation projects across the Pacific Northwest.
